How a 2-3 Tree works

A 2-3 Tree is a balanced multiway search tree. Every internal node is either a 2-node (1 key, 2 children) or a 3-node (2 keys, 3 children). All leaves are at the same depth, guaranteeing O(log n) height.

Insertion rule: Insert into a leaf. If the leaf already has 2 keys, it temporarily becomes a 4-node, then splits: the middle key is promoted to the parent and the remaining keys form two 2-nodes. The split may cascade all the way to the root, increasing the tree height by one.
