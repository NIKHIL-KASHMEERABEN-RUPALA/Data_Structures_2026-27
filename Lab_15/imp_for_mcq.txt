  EXAM MATE IMPORTANT MCQ CONCEPTS (POSTFIX ANE PREFIX MIX)

  
  1. STACK NO UPAYOG (APPLICATION OF STACK)
     - Infix mathi Postfix ke Prefix banavva mate Stack data structure vapray chhe.
     - Expression evaluation ane conversion banne ma Stack no use thay chhe.
  
  2. BRACKETS NI VALUE (PARENTHESES RULE)
     - Postfix ke Prefix na final answer ma KYAREY PAN '(' ke ')' aavta nathi.
     - Brackets khali evaluation no order change karva mate j vapray chhe.
  
  3. RANK NU SCHEME (RANKING CRITERIA FOR MCQs)
     - Rank khali check karva mate chhe ke expression valid chhe ke nahi.
     - Operands (a, b, c, 1, 2) no rank hammeša +1 hoy chhe.
     - Binary Operators (+, -, , /) no rank hammeša -1 hoy chhe.
     - Jo koi expression no total final rank exactly 1 na thay, toh te INVALID chhe.
  
  4. F ANE G FUNCTION NO TAFAVAT (INPUT VS STACK PRECEDENCE)
     - F(symbol) etle ke bahar thi aavta character ni priority.
     - G(symbol) etle ke stack ni andar bethela character ni priority.
     - Postfix ma '(' ni input priority high (9) hoy chhe jethi te stack ma jaldhi jatu rahe,
       pan stack ni andar aavya pachi teni priority low (0) thai jay chhe jethi te bija operators ne push thava de.
     - Prefix ma aakha rules reverse thai jay chhe karan ke aakhri ma string ne reverse karvani hoy chhe.
  
  5. PRECEDENCE HIERARCHY (COMPILER PRIORITY ORDER)
     - Highest priority: Exponentiation '^' (Power)
     - Medium priority: Multiplication '' ane Division '/'
     - Lowest priority: Addition '+' ane Subtraction '-'
  
  6. ASSOCIATIVITY NO TRAP (LEFT-TO-RIGHT VS RIGHT-TO-LEFT)
     - +, -, , / badha Left-to-Right associative chhe.
     - '^' (Power) hammeša Right-to-Left associative chhe, je MCQs ma varamvar puchay chhe!
